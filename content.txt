# PAGE 4 — EMAIL VERIFICATION

**Route:** `/features/email-verification`

**Hero Heading:**

Every Great Campaign Starts with a Cleaner List.

**Subheading:**

Identify invalid addresses, risky email patterns, disposable accounts, and potential delivery concerns before your next campaign.

**Primary CTA:** Verify Emails

**Secondary CTA:** Explore Bulk Verification

## THE PROBLEM

**Heading:** Bad Email Data Costs More Than You Think.

Invalid and outdated contacts can increase bounce rates, reduce campaign efficiency, and create avoidable sending risks.

Email verification gives marketers a clearer picture of list quality before launching outreach.

## HOW VERIFICATION WORKS

**Step 1:** Upload a CSV file or enter an individual email address.

**Step 2:** Check address syntax and domain formatting.

**Step 3:** Validate domain DNS and MX records.

**Step 4:** Identify disposable and role-based email addresses.

**Step 5:** Assess catch-all domains and other mailbox risk indicators.

**Step 6:** Calculate verification and risk classifications.

**Step 7:** Review results and export a cleaner audience list.

## RESULT CATEGORIES

**Valid**

The address passes the available verification checks.

**Invalid**

The address fails one or more required validation checks.

**Role-Based**

The email appears associated with a shared or functional inbox, such as sales@ or support@.

**Disposable**

The address belongs to a known temporary email provider.

**Catch-All**

The domain may accept messages for many or all local parts, making mailbox existence uncertain.

**Risky**

The address has signals that suggest additional delivery caution.

**Important Note:** Verification improves list quality but cannot guarantee mailbox existence, successful delivery, or inbox placement.

# PAGE 16 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Route:** `/help`

## What is NexaReach?

NexaReach is an AI-powered omnichannel marketing platform designed to bring lead intelligence, email marketing, WhatsApp communication, social publishing, workflow automation, and analytics into a connected workspace.

## Who is NexaReach for?

NexaReach is designed for startups, marketing teams, agencies, SaaS businesses, sales teams, and organizations looking to simplify their marketing operations.

## Can I verify emails in bulk?

The planned email verification module supports uploading CSV lists, processing batches, reviewing risk classifications, and exporting results.

## Does email verification guarantee successful delivery?

No. Email verification can identify certain address and domain risks, but it cannot guarantee mailbox existence, successful message delivery, or inbox placement.

## Can I automate email follow-ups?

NexaReach's automation capabilities are designed to support scheduled sequences and rule-based follow-ups for eligible contacts.

## Does NexaReach support WhatsApp Business?

The WhatsApp module is designed around Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform. Availability depends on supported integration access, account eligibility, and applicable platform rules.

## Can I manage multiple social media platforms?

NexaReach's social publishing vision includes supported integrations for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and X. Available actions depend on authorized API permissions and supported account types.

## Can AI create campaigns?

The AI Campaign Agent is designed to convert marketing goals into draft campaign plans, including content suggestions, audience ideas, and workflow recommendations.

## Is NexaReach suitable for agencies?

The platform is intended to support agencies managing campaigns, lead data, communication workflows, and marketing analytics. Dedicated multi-client controls should be described separately when available.

## How does NexaReach handle unsubscribes?

The email marketing system is designed to maintain unsubscribe and suppression records so future marketing sends can respect recipient preferences.

## Will I need external accounts for social and WhatsApp integrations?

Yes. Most channel integrations require an eligible external account and authorization through the relevant platform.

## Does NexaReach support customer data privacy?

NexaReach is designed around privacy-conscious data handling, communication preferences, and access controls. Specific compliance commitments should be published only after the relevant policies and safeguards have been implemented and reviewed.

## Can I track marketing performance?

The analytics modules are designed to consolidate available campaign, messaging, workflow, and engagement reporting from connected services.
